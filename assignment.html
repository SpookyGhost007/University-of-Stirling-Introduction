Canvas

Stir uni canvas is a cloud-based learning management System for current undergraduate and Postgraduate students and Course instructors at the University of Stirling to get access to course information, lecture slides, Course Announcements, and Student grades score.

If you are a student in the University of Stirling, Canvas will be your best friend during the entire study period. All the lectures, practicals, course schedule, quizzes, assignments, discussion forums and many other things will be available on Canvas.

University App

The University of Stirling app provides real-time, on-the-go, anytime information to support you during your time at Stirling. It is available to all students and staff for free.

You can access your digital student or staff card, book gym and exercise classes, order and pay for food on campus, book library study space and many more.
